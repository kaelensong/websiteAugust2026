CCBST Logo Animation

A logo reveal for California College of Business, Science and Technology, meant to reflect the accessibility and adaptability of online learning for today's on-the-go student. Designed to convey modernity and academic authority.

Client

California College of Business, Science and Technology

Role

Motion Designer, Vector Artist

Tools

Illustrator, After Effects, Photoshop

Year

2026

The Project

CCBST is a college that is currently going through a lot of changes, expanding from an online college to an in-person accredited college. With this transition, there have been several new graphics and branding changes.

I was able to help start this new chapter for CCBST by creating logo animations and logo vectorizations. Their brand emphasizes a convenient, accessible education that empowers students.

Process & Concept

First Logo Reveal

I initially worked with California College of Business, Science and Technology to do a simple logo reveal.

New Logo, New Animations

They then changed their logo and hired me to help vectorize their new logo design. I also worked to create a couple new logo animations.

A second logo animation created alongside the vectorized brand assets, continuing the refreshed look for CCBST.
