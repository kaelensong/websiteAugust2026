04 / Motion Design

Apocalypse Video Game UI

An animated UI sequence for a fictional post-apocalyptic video game, built entirely in After Effects. Combines hand-illustrated assets, real green screen footage, and 2D animation to simulate a fully playable game interface.

Client

Just for fun

Role

Creative Director, Motion Designer, Video Editing

Tools

After Effects

Year

2024

The Project

Creative Direction: a "retro" game, abandoned for many years, is rediscovered sometime in the future — this project imagines what that futuristic player finds.

Process & Concept

Character Section Screen

To set up the "character section" screen, I created a 3D environment in After Effects setting up a kind of "lazy susan" (null object) on which the characters can rotate on. This creates the effect that the video game user is scrolling through the different characters available to play as.
