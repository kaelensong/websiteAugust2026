Interactive Halloween Experience

A production case study: an interactive Halloween event combining live performance, embedded digital media and interactive narrative, produced end to end from scriptwriting to live execution.

Project

Interactive Halloween Experience & Performance

Role

Producer, Technical Lead, Co-Director & Co-Writer

Focus

Production Management, AV Setup, Video Editing

Year

2025

Overview

A few friends and I thought it would be fun to put together an interactive Halloween experience. We combined live performances, embedded digital media and interactive narrative elements. The concept of the halloween experience was that through a series of clues and events, attendees would slowly learn that there was something strange (and hopefully a bit scary) unfolding around them. The night would escalate with a series of these strange things showing up at the door.

Key Contributions

My main role was overseeing the production pipeline -- making sure everything that needed to get done got done (costume productioin, equipment procurement, multi-site AV setup, etc.).

Production Management & Operations

I built and maintained the master task tracking and logistics framework to manage timelines across prop fabrication, media acquisition, equipment checkout, stage management handoffs, and neighbor/venue communications.
